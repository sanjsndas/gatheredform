    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the most important principle in interior design?
    The most important principle is often considered to be balance—visual, spatial, and functional. However, a close second is light. Without a thoughtful approach to natural and artificial light, even the best furniture and materials fall flat. Good design begins with understanding how a space is used and how light moves through it over the course of a day.
    How do I choose the right color palette for a small room?
    For small rooms, consider a monochromatic or analogous palette with subtle variations in tone. Light, warm neutrals—like off-white, sand, or pale grey—tend to reflect light and make spaces feel larger. Avoid strong contrasts on the walls; instead, use texture and a few accent pieces to add interest. Keeping the ceiling and walls close in tone can also visually lift the ceiling.
    What are the key elements of sustainable interior design?
    Key elements include: specifying materials with low embodied carbon and low toxicity (e.g., FSC-certified wood, natural lime plaster, wool); designing for longevity and repairability; prioritizing natural ventilation and daylight; and reducing waste during construction and furnishing. True sustainability also considers the social and economic lifespan of a space—how adaptable it is to future needs.
    How many layers of lighting should a room have?
    A well-lit room typically has three to four layers: ambient (general light), task (focused light for reading or cooking), accent (highlighting art or architecture), and decorative (a sculptural pendant or candlelight). Dimmers on all layers are essential for flexibility. The goal is to avoid relying on a single ceiling fixture, which creates flat, unflattering light.
    What is the ideal circulation space in a living room?
    The general rule is to allow 30–36 inches (75–90 cm) of clear space for main walkways and 18–24 inches (45–60 cm) between furniture pieces for secondary circulation. The primary path through a room should be at least 36 inches wide. These dimensions ensure comfort and ease of movement without making the room feel empty.
