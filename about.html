    • About the Journal

    Monochrome & Space is an independent editorial platform devoted to the quiet, considered aspects of interior design and architecture.

    Our Mission

    We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical essays for designers, architects, and homeowners who care about the details. In a media landscape saturated with quick trends and disposable inspiration, we prioritise depth, material knowledge, and the principles that make spaces endure.

    Every article is written with the belief that good design is not about decoration, but about how a room works—how light moves through it, how materials age, how people live within its walls. We explore the intersections of craft, sustainability, and daily life.

    “We write for readers, not algorithms. Our aim is to inform, not to overwhelm.”

    What We Cover

    • Interior Design — colour, texture, layering, and the psychology of space.
    • Architecture — residential typologies, small lots, light, and air.
    • Materials — lime plaster, raw oak, unpolished stone, and honest finishes.
    • Lighting — the four layers, colour temperature, and fixture placement.
    • Space Planning — circulation, zoning, and the 3-foot rule.
    • Furniture — slow design, joinery, and the case for fewer, better pieces.
    • Modern Homes — warmth without sterility, minimalism without coldness.
    • Small Spaces — compact living, clever storage, and generous light.
    • Sustainable Design — longevity, repairability, and low-impact specification.

    Editorial Independence

    Monochrome & Space is entirely independent. We do not accept sponsored content, and we do not run advertising that compromises our editorial judgement. Our recommendations are based on research, experience, and a genuine interest in the built environment.

    We are reader-supported. If you value our work, the best way to support us is to subscribe to our weekly newsletter and share our articles with others who might find them useful.

    Our Team

    We are a small, distributed editorial team with backgrounds in architecture, interior design, and journalism. Our contributors are based across Europe and North America, bringing a range of perspectives to every piece we publish.

    How We Work

    Every article begins with a question. We research, interview where necessary, and draft slowly. We fact-check dimensions, material claims, and historical references. We believe that design writing should be as considered as the spaces it describes.

    Our publishing schedule is deliberately unhurried. We would rather publish two thoughtful articles a month than ten superficial ones.

    Contact & Contributions

    We welcome pitches from writers, designers, and architects. If you have a story idea or would like to contribute, please visit our contact page or email us at editorial@monochromeandspace.com.

    For press enquiries, please use the same address and include “Press” in the subject line.
