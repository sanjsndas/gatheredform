    • Cookies Policy

    This policy explains how Monochrome & Space uses cookies and similar technologies to recognise you when you visit our website. It also explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

    Last updated: 15 January 2025

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give site owners information about how their site is being used.

    Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by a domain other than our own).

    2. How we use cookies

    We use cookies for several reasons, which can be grouped into the following categories:

    • Strictly necessary cookies: These are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these cookies.
    • Performance and analytics cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve our site and content.
    • Functionality cookies: These allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your language or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features.
    • Targeting or advertising cookies: We do not currently use advertising or targeting cookies. Should this change, we will update this policy and seek your consent where required.

    3. Specific cookies we use

    Below is a detailed list of the cookies we may set and their purposes. Note that third-party cookies are subject to the respective third party's privacy policies.

    Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
    session_id Strictly necessary Maintains your session state across page requests. Session
    csrf_token Strictly necessary Helps protect against cross-site request forgery attacks. Session
    _ga Analytics (third-party) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users and track site usage. 2 years
    _gid Analytics (third-party) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users. 24 hours
    cookie_consent Functionality Stores your cookie preferences to avoid asking again. 1 year

    Note: We do not use any advertising or retargeting cookies. If we introduce them in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent where required by law.

    4. Third-party cookies

    Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. For example, we may use Google Analytics to help us measure traffic and usage trends. These third parties may set their own cookies and have their own privacy policies. We do not control the setting of these cookies, so please check the relevant third-party websites for more information.

    • Google Analytics privacy policy

    5. Your choices regarding cookies

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

    • Browser controls: Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to block or delete cookies, but please note that some parts of our website may not function properly without them.
    • Opt-out tools: You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
    • Our consent banner (if applicable): If we display a cookie consent banner, you can manage your preferences there.

    For more information about how to manage cookies in your specific browser, please refer to your browser's help documentation.

    6. Do Not Track signals

    Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Our website does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no industry consensus on how to interpret them. We will continue to monitor developments in this area.

    7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
